STAFF REFERENCE

DISCORD COMMANDS

Staff commands used to manage and moderate the Da Boyz Discord server.

BACK TO ADMIN DASHBOARD

IMPORTANT

ALL COMMANDS GO THROUGH KARODEVGROUP BOT

These commands are intended to be executed through KaroDevGroup. Do not attempt to manually replicate or bypass KaroDevGroup's moderation systems.

GENERAL COMMANDS

/version

TRIAL STAFF STAFF SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/version

Displays the current version of the Da Boyz Discord bot.

/download

TRIAL STAFF STAFF SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/download

Provides the current DaBoyzApp download or installation information.

VOICE COMMANDS

/joincall

TRIAL STAFF STAFF SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/joincall

Joins the specified Discord voice channel.

CHANNEL COMMANDS

/lock

SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/lock

Locks a channel to prevent members from sending messages.

/unlock

SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/unlock

Removes a channel lock and allows members to send messages again.

/clear

STAFF SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/clear [number_of_messages]

Deletes a specified number of messages from a channel.

/slowmode

STAFF SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/slowmode [time]

Sets or changes the slowmode delay for a channel.

MODERATION COMMANDS

/timeout

TRIAL STAFF STAFF SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/timeout [user] [duration] [reason]

Temporarily restricts a user from interacting in the Discord server.

/kick

STAFF SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/kick [user] [reason]

Kicks a user from the Discord server.

/warn

TRIAL STAFF STAFF SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/warn [user] [reason]

Warns a user and records the warning.

/warnings

TRIAL STAFF STAFF SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/warnings [user]

Displays a user's recorded warnings.

/warn_remove

STAFF SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/warn_remove [user] [warning_number]

Removes a warning from a user's warning record.

/ban

SENIOR STAFF MANAGEMENT OWNER
/ban [user] [reason]

Bans a user from the Discord server.

/unban

MANAGEMENT OWNER
/unban [user_id]

Removes a ban from a user.
