COMMUNITY STANDARDS

These rules apply to everyone in the Da Boyz community.

Staff should use these guidelines when handling reports, moderation situations, and other community issues.

RULES

RULE #1

Don't be a dick.

Treat people with respect. No harassment, discrimination, or witch hunting is tolerated. Treat others how you want to be treated.

RULE #2

No Spam/Self Promo.

No spam, server invites, or promotions without approval from staff. If you are a streamer, you are exempt from promotions.

RULE #3

Respect Privacy.

Do not DM or add other members without their permission. Do not share personal information that is not yours with other people.

RULE #4

Follow channel rules.

Use channels for their intended purposes. (Ex. Do not intentionally post memes in the music channel.)

RULE #5

No bypassing restrictions.

Do not use modified clients (e.g., Vencord) or any exploits to access/view locked channels or hidden content.

RULE #6

Listen to staff.

Staff decisions are final and not to be spoken of unless with the Owner.

STAFF NOTE

Use the Punishment Slate alongside these rules when determining appropriate consequences.
